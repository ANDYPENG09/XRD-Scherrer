Crystallite Size Distribution (CSD) Inversion — Reciprocal Space Spherical Kernel + Tikhonov-NNLS & Krill–Birringer

Isolates the selected peak by subtracting the fitted baseline and neighboring overlapping sub-peaks (or uses the full composite profile for co-centered bimodal peaks), maps to s = 2sinθ/λ − s₀, and inverts spherical crystallite diffraction kernels I_S(s; D) via volume/area-hybrid second-order Tikhonov-NNLS, cross-checked against de Keijser (1982) + Krill–Birringer lognormal analysis.
